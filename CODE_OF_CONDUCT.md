# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community for everyone, regardless
of background or identity.

Examples of expected behavior include being considerate, communicating
constructively, respecting differing viewpoints, and giving useful feedback.
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing another
person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments and contributions
that violate this policy. To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainer
through [GitHub](https://github.com/Fabr1ce). Please do not use the public
issue tracker for sensitive reports.

This policy applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public.
